Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about Paradise Fitness.

What are your opening hours?

We are open from 5:00 AM to 11:00 PM, Monday through Saturday. On Sundays, we are open from 6:00 AM to 2:00 PM.

Do you provide personal training?

Yes! We have certified personal trainers specializing in weight loss, muscle gain, CrossFit, and mobility. You can book sessions at the front desk or via our website.

Is the FitFruit Diet Plan included in the membership?

FitFruit Hub is our official diet partner. While some premium annual plans include weekly diet bowls, they are generally available for purchase separately at a discounted rate for members.

Can I freeze my membership?

Yes, members on the 6-Month and 1-Year plans can freeze their membership for up to 30 days due to travel or medical reasons.
